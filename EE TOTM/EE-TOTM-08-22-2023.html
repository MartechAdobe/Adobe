Adobe Target Test of the Month
A monthly showcase of inspiring innovations in testing.
 
     
     
 
     
 
 
EE conducted an experiment to optimise the user experience on their website's basket page. Initially, there were two "Continue shopping" CTAs, with most visitors clicking on the above-the-line CTA and leaving the basket page.
 
     
 
     
   
     
  The test aimed to assess if more people will move to the checkout page if the above-the-line "Continue shopping" CTA is removed from the basket page.  
     
 
 
 
Control
 
The basket page with 'Continue shopping' CTA above the line.
 
 
     
 
 
 
Variant
 
The basket page without the above-the-line ‘Continue shopping' CTA.
 
 
 
 
     
 
     
 
Goal
Reduce percentage of visitors clicking on the top ‘Continue shopping’ CTA and leaving the basket
 
     
 
Solution
Remove the duplicate ‘Continue shopping’ CTA at the top of the page to encourage visitors to click on ‘go to checkout’ CTA
 
     
 
Result
• +2% increase in 'Go to checkout' clicks
• +2% increase in orders (forecasted)
