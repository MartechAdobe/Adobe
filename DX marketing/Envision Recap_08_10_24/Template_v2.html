SUMMARY
Adobe Experience Makers Content Supply Chain Bootcamp – Lake Oconee | GA | September 16-18
Adobe hosted the final event of our inaugural Adobe Experience Makers Content Supply Chain Bootcamp series in Atlanta for 46 attendees from 18 accounts representing $73.5M in stage 3 & 4 pipeline.
Day 1 featured highly rated sessions from our Tech Validation, Expert and Transformational SCs who guided attendees through common challenges in the Content Supply Chain and offered practical insights through hands-on labs that showcased how to optimize workflows, plan and manage asset creation.
On Day 2, participants were introduced to Adobe Firefly and Content Activation, with previews of Content Analytics and GenStudio for Performance Marketers.
Our four-city bootcamp series included three events focused on Content Supply Chain and one on Personalization at Scale. Through the series we engaged 170 customers from 79 accounts, impacting $168M in pipeline.
We applaud the invaluable contributions from our subject matter experts. As we plan for additional value-based progression events, we will apply survey feedback from the series and look forward to launching the FY25 round of Bootcamps in early Q1.
