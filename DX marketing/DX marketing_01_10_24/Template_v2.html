SUMMARY
Adobe Experience Makers Content Supply Chain Bootcamp – Bay Area, August 19-21
Last week Adobe hosted our 2nd value-based progression Bootcamp and our 1st focused on Content Supply Chain. 45 attendees from 19 accounts representing $53M in stage 3 & 4 pipeline came together for two days of hands-on labs and peer-to-peer networking.
Day 1, hosted by our Tech Validation, Expert SCs and Transformational SCs focused on laying the foundation of CSC through hands on labs focused on workflows, planning, and asset creation. Day 2 labs went deep on Firefly, Delivery & Activation labs, customer presentations from Hertz & sneak peaks of Content Analytics and GenStudio for Performance Marketers.
CSC is a massive concept for a 2-day Bootcamp, and we received customer feedback that it was great content but hard to digest in a compacted manner and would benefit from a slower pace of delivery. As we look to continuously improve the customer experience, Adobe’s technical SME’s are incorporating that feedback into the upcoming Michigan (Sep 9-11) and Atlanta (Sept 16-18) Bootcamps.
