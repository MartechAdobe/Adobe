Summary  
SUMMARY
Adobe kicked off the first of four value-based progression Bootcamps in the United States on Aug 5-7th. 45 attendees from 27 accounts representing $133M in stage 3 & 4 pipeline went deep on Personalization at Scale (P@S) via hands on labs and strategy sessions from Adobe Technical & Solution Consulting Experts. Customers appreciated the easy access to Adobe experts, and technical labs (covering Identities, Profiles, Audiences & Activations, CJA & AJO) provided valuable insights into their current product journeys.
Our first Bootcamp was located 45 minutes north of NYC. Hosting Bootcamps outside a major city provides attendees an immersive experience, no distractions, with time to complete labs and build personal connections. Customer survey feedback consistently highlighted the event’s importance for networking with Adobe users and experts.
As we look to continuously improve the customer experience, we will be sending surveys to registrants based on lab topics two weeks before the event - addressing goals, challenges and capabilities - to ensure customers’ key themes & topics are fully covered. And we will integrate top use cases into the first portion of the labs to help set the stage.
