8 WEEKS TO SUMMIT
Get ready for an extraordinary experience as Adobe Summit returns to Las Vegas in 2024, promising to be bigger and better than ever before! We are thrilled to share some exciting highlights of what we have planned for Professional Services and Ultimate Success.
 
Summit Overview
 
 
Pre-Summit Customer Event
For the first time, we are hosting a pre-summit event – the Adobe Innovation Exchange. This invitation-only event will bring together 150+ top customers for a series of engaging activities, including keynote presentations, vision-to-value expert sessions, peer-to-peer roundtables, customer innovation panel, followed by a reception Check out what’s in store here.
 
 
Adobe Booth
Our booth will be a hub of activity, with the theme "Build Confidence in Your Success Journey with Professional Services, Ultimate Success, and Experience League." As our customers navigate Summit, we aim to provide not only knowledge about our suite of products but also a clear understanding of the services and support available for their success. The booth experience promises to deliver insights into free services, premium offerings, and more.
 
 
Breakout Sessions
Dive into 200+ breakout sessions where our subject matter experts will share the knowledge they’ve acquired while working with top brands and top customers. Visit the Summit Session Catalog for more detailed information on the sessions and speakers.
 
 
VIP Customer Experience
Elevating our customer’s Summit experience with VIP treatment. Professional Services will host an exclusive dinner for 60 people, our Ultimate Success will host an exclusive Sphere VIP experience for 30 people, and our EMEA/JAPAC teams will host a reception for their customers making this a truly global event!
 
 
Customer Briefing Center
We’re upping our game this year in the reserved meeting area by running some of our latest strategic workshops, enhancing on-site collaboration. Reserve through the Jifflenow tool. Jifflenow enablement is scheduled for Feb 8 for those who are responsible for scheduling customer meetings: Client Partners, Professional Services Sales, Ultimate Success Sales Specialists, etc. Visit the CBC Meetings sharepoint site for more information.
 
Stay tuned for more updates in coming weeks. Let’s make Adobe Summit 2024 the best experience for our customers and ourselves!
