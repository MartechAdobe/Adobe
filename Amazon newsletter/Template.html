AEP is on AWS
 
  Adobe and Amazon Web Services (AWS) have expanded their partnership to bring the Adobe Experience Platform (AEP) to AWS. This collaboration enables brands to enhance customer engagement through precise personalization and AI-driven insights, offering a unified view of customers across channels.
With applications like Adobe Real-Time CDP, Adobe Journey Optimizer, and Adobe Customer Journey Analytics, brands can create, engage, and analyze marketing content efficiently. The partnership provides brands with greater flexibility and scalability, leveraging AWS's robust cloud infrastructure for secure and seamless activation of customer insights! Learn more here
