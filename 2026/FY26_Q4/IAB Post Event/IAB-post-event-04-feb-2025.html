Adobe Summit
Read online
 
From January 26-28, 2025, we gathered in Palm Springs for three days of dynamic discussions, panels, and breakout sessions at IAB ALM. Industry leaders came together to explore the latest trends, technologies, and insights shaping the future of digital advertising, marketing, and media.
 
Adobe’s presence at IAB ALM offered a unique opportunity to engage with key decision-makers and connect with larger, more strategic customers. This event provides a platform to build meaningful relationships, foster collaboration, and expand partnerships with influential brands, helping to drive impactful growth and innovation.
 
As a Premier Sponsor, Adobe played a key role in the event, including two breakout sessions. On January 27, Sam Garfield, Head of Digital Strategy, led a conversation with Jenny Yurko, VP of Data Product Strategy at Warner Bros. Discovery, where they discussed how brands and publishers are using data collaboration to optimize targeting and drive outcomes. A standout moment was Warner Bros. Discovery’s partnership with Adobe, highlighting how data collaboration is already transforming the advertising landscape.
 
On January 28, Varun Parmar, General Manager of GenStudio, participated in a panel discussing the impact of Generative AI on creative development. The session showcased real-world case studies of how AI is streamlining content creation and unlocking new possibilities for creativity in advertising.
 
In addition to the sessions, Adobe hosted an exclusive dinner at Wally’s Restaurant, providing a unique networking opportunity in an intimate setting. The evening allowed industry leaders to connect and explore key topics in digital advertising, further reinforcing Adobe’s role in driving innovation and collaboration across the industry.
