Summit to strategy: Weekly highlights and next steps.
INTERNAL ONLY
 
Executive Forum at Summit provided a premier platform for engaging with SVP+ customers and partners in a private setting during our largest event of the year. This exclusive experience facilitated high-impact discussions with Adobe executives, industry thought leaders, and peers from the world’s leading brands. In total, we engaged 234 senior executives, representing a combined $559M in DX GNARR impact.
 
This year’s global program included executives from 172 unique accounts across 21 countries, with 77% of the attendees new to the program this year. Guests benefited from a curated agenda featuring strategic VIP networking opportunities, 1:1 meetings, a private lounge, and exclusive content hosted by Adobe executives, innovative brands, and renowned industry luminary Adam Grant, organizational psychologist and bestselling author.
 
Key themes discussed during exclusive content programming and VIP engagements centered around Organizational Readiness to Drive Transformation, Powering Growth Through C-Suite Collaboration, CMO Evolution and The Future of Marketing, Reimagining the Creative + Marketing Partnership and CIO Leadership in the Age of Digital Innovation. These sessions encourage candid discussions, fostering deeper executive relationships and actionable insights. We look forward to continuing many of these conversations at Cannes Lions.
