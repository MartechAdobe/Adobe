SUMMARY
Adobe Content Supply Chain Bootcamp – Fort Worth – June 12-13
On June 12-13, Adobe hosted a Content Supply Chain Bootcamp in Fort Worth, TX where 51 attendees from 22 accounts representing $53.3M in stage 3 & 4 pipeline came together for a day and a half of hands-on labs and peer-to-peer networking.
The event provided attendees with immersive, hands-on experiences, customer-led showcases, and meaningful discussions centered around improving content creation, collaboration, and personalization using Adobe technology and Generative AI. Customer speakers from GE Healthcare & WM—highlighted practical applications, challenges, and successes across operations, creative, and performance marketing roles. Attendees participated in persona-based labs and collaborative conversations to deepen understanding, optimize workflows, and identify actionable next steps. Team-building networking opportunities enhanced the attendee experience, featuring a lively guacamole-making battle for the best presentation and taste, followed by dinner on-site.
Key Event Highlights:
• Hands-on experience optimizing workflows and collaboration using Adobe Workfront and Adobe Experience Manager Assets.
• Practical integration of Generative AI (Adobe Firefly, Adobe GenStudio for Performance Marketing) to accelerate and scale personalized content.
• Peer discussions fostering cross-functional alignment, prioritization, and actionable takeaways.
• Exclusive preview of upcoming Adobe product innovations, maintaining momentum post-event.
