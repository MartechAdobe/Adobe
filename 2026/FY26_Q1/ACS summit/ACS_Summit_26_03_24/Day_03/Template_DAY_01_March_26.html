Professional Service leads with value.
While the big picture of GenAI, content supply chain, and customer journeys played out on the main stage, our experts on the floor brought the knowledge of how to make the vision become a reality for customers. We had 10 interactive digital whiteboards running and a large center table dedicated to live problem-solving. Customers were delighted with the breadth of content ranging from content supply chain and real-time activation at scale ROI calculators, interactive use case from MLB, end-to-end architecture, and deep dives into GenAI.
 
 
 
Ultimate Success raises the bar on support.
The mission of amplifying performance and realizing the potential for Adobe customers was on full display today. The buzz was around the beta launch of the Ultimate Success mobile app. Customers received hands-on demos of the features and functions that can help them stay on the success track along with providing instant feedback for our developers. The ‘we’re there for you’ message of Ultimate Success was further reinforced with interactive displays of customer success stories, the mechanics of how Adobe supports a customer’s organizations, and top engineers providing live help on the spot.
