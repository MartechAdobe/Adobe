7 WEEKS TO SUMMIT
This weekly newsletter will keep you informed about what Professional Services and Ultimate Success have going on at Summit 2024.
 
Register for Summit
 
The Employee Registration tool opened on Tuesday, Feb 6. If you were approved to attend Summit, you should have received your invitation to register (excluding speakers and booth staff). Your link to register is unique and cannot be shared with anyone else.
 
Take action now, if you haven’t done so already:
  • If you’ve received the invitation, you must register for Summit before Feb 16.
  • Hotels are available on a first-come, first-served basis. Please book as early as possible as rooms will most likely sell out before Feb 16.
  • All airfares must be booked and expensed by Friday, February 16 to hit Q1 expenses.
 
If your plans change, and you can no longer attend Summit, please let your manager and Kim Beahen know immediately and you will be unregistered.
 
Engaging with Customers in the Adobe Booth
 
Invite your customers to the Adobe booth! Engage your customers with our Professional Services and Ultimate Success experts on activating Adobe technology, operationalizing use cases and bringing innovation to their business.
 
 
Professional Services
Our Professional Services experts will be driving conversations across 2 major themes: Supercharge your content with GenAI and Accelerate data & journey maturity. Your customers can learn how to overcome operational challenges and realize true savings by activating new use cases.
 
 
Ultimate Success
The Ultimate Success team will be taking customers through their journey to value, demonstrating how the strategic and technical sides of the business can come together to achieve their vision. There will also be a live demo of the new Ultimate Success app and a Support Desk for customers to get immediate help on their issues from our best support engineers.
 
 
NEW! Booth Appointments
Both the Professional Services and Ultimate Success zones will be accepting booth appointments this year for a more personalized booth visit for top customers (EPIC for Professional Services and Top 50 for Ultimate Success). There is limited availability - reserve your customer’s spot today!
 
Customer Meetings at Summit
 
If you want to hold customer meetings in the Customer Briefing Center, you need to book the meeting room using the Jifflenow tool. Jifflenow enablement is scheduled on 8-Feb. Reach out to Kim Beahen if you’d like to be added to the Jifflenow enablement. If you cannot make the enablement, the session will be recorded and shared out afterwards.
 
 
Steps to prepare for customer meetings:
1. Check if your customers are registered for Summit
2. Align with license counterparts on your customer’s Summit experience.
3. Plan out meeting topics and objectives.
4. Schedule meetings with customers in Jifflenow.
Most customer meetings should be scheduled in Jifflenow by March 1.
