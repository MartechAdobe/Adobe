Activating The Hospital Contribution Fund of Australia AEP > RTCDP with Adobe Professional Services Experts
 
HCF, Australia's largest not-for-profit health fund and third-largest health insurance company by market share, aimed to address the deprecation of third-party cookies and a 77% reduction in addressable audience. They adopted Adobe's Customer Data Platform (CDP), licensing Adobe Experience Platform (AEP) with Real-Time CDP (RTCDP), to centralize and streamline audience management.
Objectives and Delivery:
HCF's goals included unifying online and offline data for a centralized view of prospects and members, developing audience segments, and activating these audiences on platforms like Google and Facebook. Adobe Professional Services implemented five use cases to demonstrate the platform’s capabilities, including high-value audience modeling, ad spend optimization, retargeting prospects, cross-selling to members, and reducing churn.
What We Delivered:
 
• Ingested datasets from HCF’s Snowflake-based data warehouse and Adobe Analytics.
• Created and activated six segments across five use cases.
• Implemented DULE labeling and supported security and legal reviews.
• Assisted with channel activations on Meta and Google.
• Provided performance testing and deskside coaching for UAT readiness.
Challenges Overcome:
The project went live within four months, despite initial expectations of six months. Key challenges included extensive 'framing' for understanding, complex data object modeling, and timeline issues for data view creation.
Next Steps:
Post go-live support is underway, with an implementation review planned. A three-month Hypercare period has been signed, and a next phase proposal is in discussion.
Project Team:
 
• Sales: Matthew Walker, Neil Kelly/Dev Behara, Patrick Silani
• Adobe Professional Services: Jed Elliot, Matt Bruty, Faraz Shah, Sunil Kumar Sahu, Mohit Jain, Cody Field, Peter Hallas, Robert Budiman, Shanal Singhal, Arijit Ghosh, Sukuna Mani, Sachit Kapoor, Cheekati Manikishore, Albertus Lim, Ketan Sharma
This project underscores HCF's dedication to using Adobe's solutions for enhanced customer engagement and business growth.

Activating ANZ Bank Adobe campaign cloud migration with Adobe Professional Services Experts
 
Adobe and ANZ Bank: Successful Campaign Migration
The Adobe team has successfully deployed three batches of campaign migration to Adobe Managed Services (AMS) for ANZ Bank in February, March, and April. This migration has significantly improved ANZ Bank's operational efficiency and reduced costs.
Key Highlights:
 
• Project Overview: ANZ Bank faced challenges with two separate Adobe Campaign instances. The goal was to migrate customer data and campaigns from the on-premises Kalido instance to the cloud-based Startech instance, ensuring scalability and efficiency.
• Delivery and Benefits: The consolidation resulted in a unified instance for both customer and prospect data, timely migration, increased email throughput, cost reduction, enhanced operational efficiency, and reduced compliance risks.
• Challenges Overcome: The project overcame a tight timeline, onboarding delays, limited access to data, compliance risks, lack of documentation, and the transition to a mid-source architecture.
• Customer Feedback: ANZ Bank praised the Adobe Professional Services team for their proactiveness, fast turnaround, and outstanding performance.
Next Steps:
 
• Post go-live support and a planned implementation review.
• A three-month Hypercare period.
• Discussions for Phase 2 delivery.
Project Team: Special congratulations to the dedicated team members in both onshore and offshore delivery teams, who played a crucial role in this successful migration.
Please contact Longgang Liang (Senior Project Manager) for more information.
