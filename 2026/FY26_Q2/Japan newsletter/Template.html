Sales & Revenue
 
 
 
  The team was lucky to have Jay in Sydney last week playing a supporting and mentoring role across the ANZ PSS Deep Dive Sessions. While in Sydney we launched the FY24 Pitch Perfect initiative. Each PSS in ANZ was tasked to prepare & present (~10min) sales presentation targeted for a CxO audience. The pitch needed to be targeted to an existing customer within their territory with the objective to create interest and a customer call to action, and to either progress existing DR or to generate new opportunity. The content for the pitch needed to leverage the content presented and shared from the FY24 APS Sales Kick-off (PIE, CSC, GenAI). It was a great opportunity for each PSS to “practise” their pitch to Jay and received very valued feedback on how to make each more impactful. Next step is to roll out the Asia PSS edition of Pitch Perfect and to make this initiative a regular event in the enablement calendar  
 
 
 
 
  Mutsumi is spearheading the transition from PSA (Professional Services Automation) to DPO (Microsoft Dynamics Project Operations) within the Professional Services organization. This initiative is not limited to Japan but extends globally. Japan is the initial region for this rollout due to its single language and currency, simplifying the migration process. The successful implementation in Japan will set the stage for the second phase of the rollout, scheduled for July, which will encompass the rest of the world. The goal of this migration is to enhance efficiency across the organization by leveraging DPO’s capabilities, such as improved project planning, resource optimization, and comprehensive financial management. If you have any questions or need assistance with the DPO system, Mutsumi is there to help.
