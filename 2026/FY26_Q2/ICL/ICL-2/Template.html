SUMMARY
Envision Atlanta | February 6 | TKE Innovation & Qualification Center
Adobe Envision 2025 in Atlanta offered a focused day of transformation, combining forward-thinking presentations with hands-on collaboration to guide 44 attendees from 21 accounts impacting $10.1M pipeline through the ‘what,’ ‘why,’ and ‘how’ of marketing innovation.
The event began with a keynote exploring key industry trends and establishing Adobe’s framework for achieving personalization at scale, setting the stage for an in-depth session on the latest product innovations. The Product Innovation Session aligned to real-world business challenges and how each elevate marketing strategies and streamline operations, including Federated Audience Composition, Real-Time CDP Collaboration, Content Hub, GenStudio for Performance Marketing, AJO B2B, Journey Canvas, and Workfront Planning.
Attendees then engaged in an expert-led workshop that helped benchmark organizational maturity, prioritize high-impact opportunities, and define actionable next steps. By the day’s end, the networking reception fostered meaningful connections and rich discussions, as attendees and Adobe experts had worked side by side throughout the event, creating a strong foundation for ongoing customer conversations and continued digital transformation.
