SUMMARY
Adobe Content Supply Chain Bootcamp – Tarrytown, NY – May 15-16
On May 15-16, Adobe hosted a Content Supply Chain Bootcamp in Tarrytown, NY where 54 attendees from 29 accounts representing $44M in stage 3 & 4 pipeline came together for a day and a half of hands-on labs and peer-to-peer networking.
The event provided attendees with immersive, hands-on experiences, customer-led showcases, and meaningful discussions centered around improving content creation, collaboration, and personalization using Adobe technology and Generative AI. Customer speakers from Tapestry/Coach — David Gormly, J.J. Camara, and Brandon Keeney—highlighted practical applications, challenges, and successes across operations, creative, and performance marketing roles. Attendees participated in persona-based labs and collaborative conversations to deepen understanding, optimize workflows, and identify actionable next steps. Team-building networking opportunities enhanced the attendee experience, featuring a lively bartending battle for the best cocktail and mocktail, followed by dinner at an onsite speakeasy.
Key Event Highlights:
• Hands-on experience optimizing workflows and collaboration using Adobe Workfront and Adobe Experience Manager Assets.
• Practical integration of Generative AI (Adobe Firefly, Adobe GenStudio for Performance Marketing) to accelerate and scale personalized content.
• Peer discussions fostering cross-functional alignment, prioritization, and actionable takeaways.
• Insightful customer perspectives from Tapestry/Coach demonstrating real-world applications and value.
• Exclusive preview of upcoming Adobe product innovations, maintaining momentum post-event.
