Orchestrating Experiences with Data, Journey and Content Intelligence
 
As we roll out our capabilities with Adobe’s solutions, we are bringing data, journey, and content together in a more connected and highly contextualized way—transforming how we engage with customers across the entire funnel. From the creation and discovery of content to how and where and by whom it’s consumed, we’re building a seamless, high-performing marketing engine. By connecting account engagement stages with dynamic content and intelligent data enrichment, we’re able to deliver personalized, scalable experiences that drive impact.
Below are just a few key highlights of how we’re using Adobe’s solutions across data, journey, and content to power marketing effectiveness.
 
 
Boosting Precision Marketing with Data Enrichment
 
We’re launching a pilot to strengthen our person data foundation with ZoomInfo data for a set of priority contacts, with the goal of improving the accuracy and completeness of information in key contact fields. This enhancement will enable precise segmentation, better personalization, and more effective campaign targeting.
Analysis has shown that ZoomInfo can enrich our internal Marketo database with 92.5% company match accuracy and over 87% accuracy in management level and job function. The enrichment gains include a 30% increase in job title coverage and notable improvements across other key fields.
New ZoomInfo fields, such as Job Title, Management Level, and Contact Accuracy Score in Real Time-CDP are under development. These power modular audience segments, multichannel activation, and strategic use cases—driving stronger campaign performance and customer engagement.
This is one of many steps we are taking to advance our data foundation through third-party enrichment. We plan to leverage LLMs for all third-party data vendor account mapping—unlocking greater efficiency and precision in marketing and sales activation.
 
 
Driving Pipeline with Precision: How AES Data Powers BDR Efficiency
 
Adobe’s integration of Account Engagement Stage (AES) data into BDR workflows has enhanced productivity and pipeline quality. By connecting AES with AEHub, DynamicsCRM, Outreach.io, and AI tools, BDRs can prioritize outreach to the right accounts and contacts at the right time. This approach boosts connect rates, minimizes dashboard toggling, and sharpens focus on high-conversion accounts.
In just a few months, BDRs increased connect rates by 4 points and generated 10 new opportunities totaling $1M in GNARR. BDR Katie Powell shared, “I never would have engaged with the CIO if I hadn’t leveraged these account alerts and done my research.” Her AES-guided outreach led to a direct CIO meeting and a new strategic relationship.
BDRs are scaling personalized outreach and uncovering opportunities, even in previously disengaged accounts. AES data is more than a signal—it’s a strategic advantage for building smarter, faster pipeline.
 
 
New frontiers: Innovating and scaling with Adobe Express
 
The India Content Labs team (ICL) has been championing the use of Adobe Express to help drive standardization and identify innovative ways to speed up the delivery of high-quality content and creatives.
To support EMEA field events, the team developed brand kits for Executive Forum, OMR, London Summit, LEAP, and Amplify so that in-region marketers can self-serve and drive velocity with a plug-and-play of all available creative assets. These kits include templates, logos, colors, and fonts, making them accessible, scalable, and ideal for empowering marketers to produce brand-compliant assets. Brand kits in Adobe Express can be applied to templates for quick customization by regional campaign teams. The team also raised the bar by delivering four persona-based Express templates (Creative, Marketing, Marketing Ops, and IT) for the Americas BDR team to use post-Summit, helping them drive an immersive account engagement experience while encouraging internal reusability of these templates for faster turnaround.
