Customers
 
BOOKINGS
We’re excited to share that DATEV has signed two renewals totaling $508k, reinforcing their commitment to advancing their ambitious roadmap on Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) and Adobe Campaign (AC) in H2’2025.
This continued collaboration, now spanning several years, is a testament to the strong and successful partnership between DATEV and Adobe. A key milestone in this journey was the recent Customer Experience Center (CEC) visit in San Jose, where a high-level DATEV delegation engaged in meaningful discussions with Anil Chakravarthy, Anjul Bhambhri, Ash King, and others. This exchange not only deepened our relationship but also led to a significant outcome: Adobe being awarded preferred vendor status. This designation means DATEV will now review Adobe’s portfolio first for future needs, enabling direct purchases without a tender process.
Special thanks goes to Marcus Rink, who has expertly developed this account over the years as PSS and co-organised the CEC visit with Nora Wittig and the broader account team. Nora’s outstanding support was instrumental in the success of the visit. The positive feedback from DATEV highlights the strength of the trusted relationship we’ve built.
We also extend our gratitude to Daniela Neske (Project Manager) and Sandro Schwedler (Delivery Sponsor) for their excellent ongoing work, and to Guy Walker and Sneha Swamy for the straightforward SO approval and release.

We are currently supporting CHANEL’s Fashion Division in building their future content supply chain. The objective is, by the end of 2026, all strategic content productions, including fashion shows, collections, and campaigns, will be orchestrated from shooting to publication within Adobe Platform. We have launched an MVP focusing on a specific use case: the digitalisation (for the first time!) of the 31 Cambon magazine (an iconic, non-commercial publication, sent exclusively to CHANEL’s top clients), scheduled for release at the end of September. At the same time, we are exploring additional use cases to prepare for a broader rollout, starting in October.
This marks our first major end-to-end content supply chain reference in the French luxury fashion sector!
Huge thanks to the incredible team behind this success, true teamwork in action - Giorgia Gili, Arnaud Antoine, Romain Alnet, Laurent Giet, Jonathan Geismar, Jérôme Chekroun, Rajeev Duggal, José Carreno, Marco Luna, Ambroise Delaporte, Arthur Bamy, and everyone else who contributed behind the scenes.
 
 
 
 
Business
 
VivaTech | 11-14 June, Paris
As the #1 tech event in EMEA, VivaTech 2025 once again proved to be the epicenter of innovation and impact, welcoming 180,000 visitors and 14,000 startups from 171 countries.
 
 
Adobe made a bold statement at the event, with Rachel Thornton, Hannah Elsakr, Varun Parmar, and Luc Dammann leading standout sessions on AI and brand experience. Exclusive CMO and CEO dinners, along with VIP tours with LVMH and L’Oréal, amplified Adobe’s strategic visibility. Our presence was powered by strong collaboration between license and services sales teams, engaging major accounts through joint business meetings, driven in part by Laurent Giet’s pivotal involvement. Check out his LinkedIn post!
 
Key highlights
• Joint business meetings with top accounts, including CHANEL, EssilorLuxottica, Hermès, Capgemini, PMI, Crédit Agricole, SNCF, L'Oréal, Amadeus, and Royal Canin.
• $11.1M in services pipeline engaged, showcasing the strength of Adobe’s collaborative approach.
 
This strategic alignment not only deepened relationships with key clients but also reinforced Adobe’s commitment to innovation and growth through unified efforts.
