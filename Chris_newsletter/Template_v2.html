Customers
 
We’re excited to announce that we’ve secured a new $106k contract with TSB, marking a significant milestone in our partnership and ongoing strategic alignment. This agreement is focused on supporting TSB’s digital transformation roadmap and addressing several key areas critical to their evolving marketing and operational capabilities.
Huge thanks to Nick Spyer, Deepti Rawat, Bronwen Peel, and Rajeev Nair for their work with TSB that led to this deal.
 
 
Deal signed with Sky for Adobe Workfront Implementation Services. This marks an exciting milestone in our growing partnership with Sky, as they embark on a significant transformation of their work management practices using Adobe Workfront. Adobe Professional Services (APS) will lead the implementation, leveraging our proven methodology and industry best practices to ensure a scalable, sustainable foundation for Sky’s long-term success. As part of this engagement, APS will also provide strategic guidance on integrations through Workfront Fusion, enabling a seamless, end-to-end work management experience across teams and processes.
Thanks to Fiona Falcone, Deepti Rawat, Amanda Sharp, Dominic Reid, Juanita Hennessey, Bronwen Peel, Mahesh Ede, and Guy Walker.
 
 
Siemens Healthineers has renewed its engagement with APS, reinforcing their commitment to digital transformation. Leveraging Adobe Experience Platform, Real-Time CDP, Adobe Target, and Adobe Customer Journey Analytics, the company aims to unify data, personalise engagement, and drive insights globally.
This continued collaboration supports scalable tech integration and aligns with Siemens’ vision for personalised, data-driven healthcare. Upcoming priorities include RTCDP enablement in China and AJO B2B activation this Autumn.
Special thanks to Benjamin van Impelen, Manan Jain, Ankit Kathuria, Lennert Rienau, Guy Walker, and Shivaansh Tiwari.

The customer aims to enhance the performance and reliability of the Vatican's two main websites.

OMR Hamburg was a resounding success, showcasing Adobe’s innovation and impact! Our booth captivated attendees, drawing in crowds and generating fantastic engagement. With a record-breaking 82 meetings and 20 highly successful Masterclasses, including "Next-Level Marketing: With Adobe Towards an Efficient Content Supply Chain," with Kojhen Lateef (110 participants) and "The Role of GenAI in Digital Marketing – Delivering Personalised and Effective Customer Journeys," with Claudius Nitsch and Henkel as customer speaker (133 participants), we made meaningful connections and sparked valuable discussions. Building Stronger Connections - Middle East
We are delighted to share a recap of a recent event that underscores Adobe Professional Services' commitment to nurturing and strengthening essential client relationships in the Middle East.
 
 
On 7 May, we hosted a relationship-building dinner with Alshaya, E&, and Mashreq Bank during a private yacht cruise along the stunning Dubai River. Gartner Marketing Symposium
We are pleased to announce that Adobe Professional Services successfully engaged customers and prospects at the Gartner Marketing Symposium in London on 12th–13th May. The event was a resounding success, showcasing the synergy between license and services marketing.
The symposium attracted over 600 participants from diverse sectors, including travel, retail, pharma, finance, and more. Key representatives from these accounts collectively represent an open services pipeline exceeding $5M.
 
 
During the event, Wendy Tarr, Senior Client Partner, Professional Services, delivered a compelling presentation on, "People, Processes, and Technology," to a full house of customers on the morning of Day 2. The presentation, titled, "AI in Action: Aligning People, Process & Strategy for Business Success," attracted an impressive 87 attendees. Melissa Feaviour, Manager, Professional Services Sales, and Yasmine Newman, Account Manager, Professional Services Sales, also attended both days, networking with our most significant accounts and forging valuable new connections for APS.
 
 
Virtual Roundtable – Move to Cloud
 
Our virtual roundtable "Breaking Barriers: Navigating Culture, Tech & Speed in Cloud Migration" brought together industry leaders from Allianz, Rewe, Payback, Fresenius, and Hello Fresh for a dynamic discussion on the future of cloud migration.
 
Led by Jonas Vanvoorden, the conversation explored how cloud can accelerate speed to market, the challenges of overcoming cultural and technical barriers, and strategies for bridging the gap between business and IT to create a unified, customer-centric approach. Recording available here.
