SUMMARY
Adobe Unified Customer Experience Bootcamp – Lake Oconee, GA – June 3-4
On June 3-4 Adobe hosted a Unified Customer Experience Bootcamp in Lake Oconee, Georgia where 50 attendees from 19 accounts representing $45M in stage 3 & 4 pipeline came together for a day and a half of hands-on labs and peer-to-peer networking.
Adobe experts and technical subject matter experts covered a mix of keynotes spotlighting the end-to-end Adobe Experience Platform and Agentic AI, as well as hands-on labs for Adobe Real-Time Customer Data Platform and Adobe Real-Time Customer Data Platform Collaboration, Adobe Journey Optimizer, and Adobe Customer Journey Analytics. Customers identified their current use cases and where they see gaps within their own marketing tech stack which set the stage for how they can approach the hands-on labs. Customers engaged in a competitive rocket-making challenge prior to enjoying dinner along the picturesque Lake Oconee.
 
Key Event Highlights:
• Previewed what Adobe’s Agentic AI Framework has in store with Adobe Experience Platform Agent Orchestrator.
• Built unified customer profiles for audience segmentation with Adobe Real-Time Customer Data Platform.
• Designed and orchestrated personal customer journeys in real-time with Adobe Journey Optimizer.
• Analyzed cross-channel customer interactions to derive actionable insights with Adobe Customer Journey Analytics.
• Identified current organizational use-cases and how the above solutions can help close the gap within their own marketing tech stack.
