Celebrating the voices and vision of top women in retail at an exclusive summit.
     
 
 
April 28-30, 2025 | The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island, FL
 
 
INTERNAL ONLY
 
Last week, the Retail and CG industry marketing team sponsored the Women in Retail Summit held at the Ritz-Carlton on Amelia Island, Florida. This invitation-only event is designed for women in leadership positions at top retailers and brands who want to learn, network, and improve their business acumen. At the conference, dynamic presentations and inspirational keynotes covered everything from leadership development to staying at the forefront of retail technology. Additionally, attendees were able to network at receptions, meals, and breakouts over the course of three days. Adobe’s sponsorship included delivering a ten-minute breakout presentation, facilitating a small lunch roundtable, and providing a Gen AI photobooth activation.
 
 
Event Details
 
Event Dates: April 28-30, 2025
Event Location: The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island, FL
Conference Link: Agenda | Women In Retail Summit
 
Lunch Roundtable: Multiple topics were featured in one room. Hannah Elsakr and Marta Frattini each hosted a table of 9 attendees to facilitate a discussion on “Using AI Responsibly: Balancing Tech and the Human Touch.”
10 Minute Sponsor Breakout Presentation: “Customer Experience in the Era of AI” presented by Marta Frattini and Hannah Elsakr. The presentation highlighted Adobe as “our own customer ground zero” and several customer success stories including Gatorade and Estee Lauder.
Adobe Activation: The Gen AI Photobooth was available throughout the conference and was a big hit! 450 attendees participated.
