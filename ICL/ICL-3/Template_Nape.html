SUMMARY
Adobe Unified Customer Experience Bootcamp – Napa, CA – May 20-21
On May 20-21 Adobe hosted a Unified Customer Experience Bootcamp in Napa, CA where 45 attendees from 16 accounts representing $28M in stage 3 & 4 pipeline came together for a day and a half of hands-on labs and peer-to-peer networking.
Adobe experts and technical subject matter experts covered a mix of keynotes spotlighting the end-to-end Adobe Experience Platform and Agentic AI, as well as hands-on labs for Adobe Real-Time Customer Data Platform and Adobe Real-Time Customer Data Platform Collaboration, Adobe Journey Optimizer, and Adobe Customer Journey Analytics. Customers identified their current use cases and where they see gaps within their own marketing tech stack which set the stage for how they can approach the hands-on labs. Customers engaged in a competitive wine-blending networking and team-building challenge, followed by dinner with a side of Bocce ball.
Key Event Highlights:
• Previewed what Adobe’s Agentic AI Framework has in store with Adobe Experience Platform Agent Orchestrator.
• Built unified customer profiles for audience segmentation with Adobe Real-Time Customer Data Platform.
• Designed and orchestrated personal customer journeys in real-time with Adobe Journey Optimizer.
• Analyzed cross-channel customer interactions to derive actionable insights with Adobe Customer Journey Analytics.
• Identified current organizational use-cases and how the above solutions can help close the gap within their own marketing tech stack.
