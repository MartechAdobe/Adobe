Amazon Ads and Adobe to enhance audience and creative optimization for advertisers
Paul Kotas, SVP, Amazon Ads
Many advertisers are unable to efficiently create and scale high-quality, personalized ad campaigns, holding them back from investing further with Amazon Ads. Their challenges primarily relate to content creation, audience targeting, and performance attribution.
Amazon Ads and Adobe are exploring integrating GenStudio PeM, Real-time CDP Collaboration, and Firefly Services with Amazon Ads' platforms to improve creative personalization, audience targeting, reporting and insights, and campaign optimization across multiple channels.
Amazon Ads and Adobe to enhance media optimization for advertisers
Paul Kotas, SVP, Amazon Ads
Advertisers are facing significant challenges in effectively utilizing their first-party onsite data while integrating their external data sources to optimizate media, audience taregeting, and creatie content. The lack of a seemless eperiences in activating and leveraging these data sets results in inefficiencies and suboptimal campaign performance.
Amazon Ads and and Adobe are partnering to innovate the future of customer acquisition paid media technology to build a central operating system that integrates advertisers first-party onsite through Adobe Analytics with Amazon's proprietary second-party data with a tightly integrated Adobe Dynamic Creative Optimization to effectively optimize media, audiences, and creative.
Selling Partner Services and Adobe to enable enterprise solutions for Amazon sellers
Mary Beth Westmoreland, VP, WW Selling Parnter Experience
Sellers need to create high quality content faster and with insight into how the content is performing so they can "fail fast" and iterate.
Selling Partner Services and Adobe are exploring integrating Adobe Express in "crawl" phase to enable high-quality listings and A+ Content for sellers. Parties will also explore Adobe GenStudio and Analytics for seller journey optimization to reach customers at the right time with the right content.
AWS and Adobe to enable web content management and digital asset management to Amazon sellers and advertisers
Matt Garman, CEO, AWS

Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) and Adobe to collaborate to modernize cover design and marketing content creation for self-published authors
Panos Panay, SVP, Devices & Services
KDP Authors struggle with creating book covers and translating them in various formats. Authors must create digital versions but also need to send to print which is a current challenge. Once the cover content is created those assets need to be leveragable for marketing and promotional content.
KDP and Adobe are discussing leveraging Firefly Services and Adobe Express to automate cover creation, adaptation for various formats and assist in generating. In the run stage, KDP Authors can leverage GenStudio PeM and activating marketing content seemlessly and at scale.
