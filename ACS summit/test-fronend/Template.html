Countdown to Summit
Prepare your customers as Adobe Summit returns to Las Vegas in 2025, promising to be more spectacular than ever! This monthly countdown will cover the top items you need to know to help your customers have the best Summit experience.
 
  Event Highlights & Key Activities
 
 
Pre-event Innovation Exchange
For the second year, ACS is hosting the Adobe Innovation Exchange. This invitation-only event will gather top ACS customers for expert sessions, strategy circles, and a customer innovation panel. Invitations are reserved for ACS customers or pipeline opportunities with Director+ titles. The landing page for this event is scheduled to launch by the end of this week, with the first wave of invites going out on February 5.
 
 
Break-out Sessions
The Summit session catalog will go live on Friday, allowing registrants to sign up for sessions. This year, ACS experts are presenting more than 20 sessions and labs. Check out the Summit Session Catalog for more detailed information on all sessions and speakers.
 
 
Customer Briefing Center
We're enhancing our reserved meeting area this year by offering some of our latest strategic workshops to boost on-site collaboration. Reservations can be made through the Jifflenow tool, which will be enabled at the beginning of February for those responsible for scheduling customer meetings: Client Partners, Professional Services Sales, Ultimate Success Sales Specialists, etc. Visit the CBC Meetings SharePoint site for more information. Additionally, the "Requesting Customer Meetings at Summit" training using the Jifflenow tool is scheduled for February 6.
 
 
ACS Booth
Under construction 🚧 stay tuned for more details on the exciting activities and showcases planned for the ACS booth at Summit.
 
 
Customer Events
Details and invitations launching soon for the following ACS exclusive events:
• Day 0: Innovation exchange for Director+ contacts
• Day 1: ACS Customer dinner for $500k+ opportunities
• Day 2: ACS VIP event for top renewal opportunities
 
Stay Tuned for more updates next month!
 
  Employee Zone
 
  • Nominations: In coordination with your leaders, nominations have been finalized for most ACS attendees.
  • Registration: The employee registration tool will open the week of February 3.
  • Invitation: If you are approved to attend Summit, you will receive an invitation letter during the week of February 3 with instructions on how to book your hotel and travel. Please do not make any arrangements until you receive this email invitation.
  • Deadlines: The deadline to register and submit expense reports is February 14.
  • Promoting Key Sessions: Attention all CSMs, TAMs, and professional services consultants! Scott has requested your support in promoting and registering for sessions related to Adobe's agent-based strategy and AI assistance at the Summit. Your participation is crucial to ensure these important sessions are well-attended and impactful.
