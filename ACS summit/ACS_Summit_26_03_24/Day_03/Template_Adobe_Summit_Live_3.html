We Closed a Deal
 
American Chemical Society stopped by the Commerce Made Personal zone for an interactive whiteboard architecture review and ended up coming back every day this week. They are Adobe Experience Manager and Commerce customers but had not invested in Adobe Professional Services. As a result of their conversations at the Booth, they have signed up for a Commerce Health Check service at $50K, with a long-term opportunity to move to cloud and integrate Experience Manager and Commerce.
